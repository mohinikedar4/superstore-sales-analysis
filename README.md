# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales, profit, discounts, customers, products, categories, and regional performance using the Superstore dataset.

The goal of this analysis is to identify key business trends, understand profitability, detect loss-making areas, and provide data-driven recommendations that can help improve business performance.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Technology is the strongest category

Technology generated approximately **$836K in sales** and **$145K in profit**, with a profit margin of **17.40%**.

### 2. Furniture has a profitability problem

Furniture generated approximately **$742K in sales**, but its profit was only **$18.45K**, resulting in a low **2.49% profit margin**.

This indicates that high sales volume does not necessarily translate into strong profitability.

### 3. Tables is the biggest loss-making sub-category

Tables generated approximately **$207K in sales** but recorded a loss of approximately **$17.73K**.

Tables had an overall profit margin of approximately **-8.56%**.

### 4. Higher discounts are associated with lower profitability

The analysis showed a strong relationship between high discount levels and declining profit margins.

For example:

* 0% discount → 29.51% profit margin
* 30% discount → -10.05%
* 40% discount → -19.81%
* 70% discount → -98.66%
* 80% discount → -180.03%

These results suggest that discounting and pricing strategies should be reviewed, particularly for loss-making products.

### 5. Regional performance differs for Tables

The East region was the weakest region for the Tables sub-category, with approximately **-$11K in profit**.

The West region was the only region where Tables generated a positive profit.

### 6. High sales do not always mean high profitability

Sean Miller was the highest-sales customer among the analyzed top customers, generating approximately **$25K in sales**, but the customer generated approximately **-$1.98K in profit**.

This demonstrates why customer analysis should consider profitability rather than revenue alone.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Review pricing and discount strategies for highly discounted products.
2. Investigate the profitability of the Furniture category.
3. Prioritize corrective action for the Tables sub-category.
4. Review Tables pricing and discounting specifically in the East region.
5. Evaluate consistently loss-making products for repricing, bundling, or discontinuation.
6. Measure customer performance using both revenue and profit rather than revenue alone.
7. Focus growth strategies on categories and products with strong profit margins.
